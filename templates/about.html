Hybrid Machine Learning Formula

Every transaction is screened in real time by two complementary models:

Risk Score (0–100) = 0.7 × (RF fraud probability × 100) + 0.3 × (Isolation Forest anomaly score × 100)
Score 0 – 39: Approve
Clean transaction. Processed immediately with zero customer disruption.
Score 40 – 69: Flag & SMS Alert
Unusual amount, location, or merchant category. Triggers SMS alert to customer.
Score 70+: Block Card & SMS Alert
Severe fraud risk or novel anomaly vector. Freezes card immediately.
Security Rule: Wrong PIN Lockout

Entering 3 consecutive incorrect PINs immediately locks the card independently of the ML score. This prevents brute-force attempts on lost or stolen cards.
